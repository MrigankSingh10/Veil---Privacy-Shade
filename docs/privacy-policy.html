VeilType Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Information VeilType uses

VeilType provides an optional privacy veil over other apps. When you enable its Android accessibility service, it observes top-level keyboard window type and bounds and the foreground app’s package name. It uses this information on your device to position the veil and apply app rules. VeilType does not traverse accessibility nodes or read text displayed or typed in other apps.

When you open app rules, VeilType displays the names and package names of launcher-visible apps on your device. It stores the rules you choose locally and uses them to decide when to show the veil.

VeilType stores settings, including app rules, schedules, appearance and control position, on your device. If you choose a photo, it stores a reference and uses Android document access to display the photo. The original stays with its document provider, whose own privacy practices apply. Android may retain document access grants until revoked or the app is uninstalled.

VeilType has no internet permission and does not include accounts, advertising or analytics. It does not send this information to the developer.

Permissions and your choices

Accessibility and display-over-other-apps access require your action in Android settings. VeilType uses them to detect the keyboard and draw the veil. You can disarm VeilType or revoke access in Android settings. Notification access controls visibility of service controls, subject to Android’s behavior. If you enable start after restart, VeilType receives Android’s boot-completed signal to restore the armed service.

Data security

VeilType stores preferences in Android’s private app storage and disables app backup. Android controls access to that storage. The app does not add a separate encryption layer for its settings.

Retention and deletion

Preferences and app rules remain on your device until changed, the app’s data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling removes local app data and Android document grants; it does not delete the original photo. A veil is a visual privacy aid and does not prevent screenshots, screen readers or cameras from capturing content.
